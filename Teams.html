Vivian's synergies

Below Vivian's best synergies will be listed

Vivian can work with others if you know what to do and how to use them.

Vivian functions very well with all the Anomaly DPS Agents we have in the game. They activate Vivian's Additional Ability and her damage scales directly off of how much Anomaly damage her teammates do. For this reason, Miyabi is the odd one out of the bunch as a lot of her damage comes from CRIT instead of Anomaly, however she still deals enough Anomaly DMG to make a viable pairing. Overall, all of Vivian's best teams involve Anomaly DPS Agents as they increase each other's damage, making these pairings highly recommended.

Astra Yao

Yuzuha

Nicole

Lucy











These 4 generalist Support units are capable of servicing as the Support to any team in the game. Astra Yao provides Quick Assists, extra Chain Attacks, healing, super low field time, ATK, DMG and CRIT DMG buffs which makes her one of the strongest overall choices. Astra Yao also helps tremendously with consuming Guard Feathers for Vivian to dish out her damage faster. Yuzuha is the ultimate Anomaly buffer, surpassing Astra Yao in those teams, who also has awesome ATK and DMG% buffs applicable to any Agent as well, giving her strong general use potential; Nicole provides the sought-after DEF Shred and Ether DMG Boost via her Additional Ability. Nicole and Astra Yao are particularly appreciated due to helping Vivian activate Corruption. Lastly, Lucy has long-lasting ATK buffs which makes her a very practical choice, and she ends up being preferable when needed to activate Additional Abilities of certain characters. All in all, these four can work with any team in the game to great effect.

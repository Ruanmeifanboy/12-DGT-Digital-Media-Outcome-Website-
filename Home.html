Description

Vivian is a 5 star character in the 3d action role-playing game (ARPG) zenless zone zero.
She is of the ether attribute specialising in Anomaly as her agent type/category.
Part of the Mocking Bird faction,Vivian is skilled in gathering all kinds of intel.
She's also a jack of many trades — cooking, mixology, translation, art forgery, electronics repair, and more.

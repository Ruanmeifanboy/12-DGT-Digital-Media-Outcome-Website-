Wengine Discs

Wengines

Vivian only has 2 viable Wengines

Flight of Fancy

Increases Anomaly Buildup Rate by 40/46/52/58/64%.
When the equipper deals Ether DMG, their Anomaly Proficiency increases by 20/23/26/29/32 for 5s, stacking up to 6 times.
This effect can trigger once every 0.5s, and repeated triggers reset the duration.

Vivian's signature W-Engine and easily her best option, as it gives her 90 Anomaly Proficiency from base stats
and an extra 120 stackable Anomaly Proficiency at full stacks while giving a passive 40% Anomaly Buildup Rate.
This boosts her Anomaly potential by huge amounts, making it the best by raw stats alone.

Weeping Gemini

Whenever a squad member inflicts an Attribute Anomaly on an enemy,
the equipper gains a buff that increases Anomaly Proficiency by 30/34/38/42/46, stacking up to 4 times.
This effect expires when the target recovers from Stun or is defeated.
The duration of each stack is calculated separately.

Weeping Gemini is F2P accessible and the next best option for her after her signature, making it her only other recommendation.
It grants her ATK% and a tonne of AP when at maximum stacks which should be often. When full team damage is considered
(not just Vivian's own damage) it performs even closer to her signature than represented, further cementing its spot as a potent F2P alternative.

Best Disk Drives Sets

Phaethon's Melody is a must

Phaethon's Melody (4pc)
(2pc) Anomaly Mastery +8%.
(4pc) When any squad member uses an EX Special Attack, the equipper's Anomaly Proficiency increases by 45 for 8s. If the character using the EX Special Attack is not the equipper, the equipper's Ether DMG is increased by 25%.

Best Disc Drive set for Anomaly-focused Ether Agents, as it gives a stackable 45 Anomaly Proficiency bonus for 8s when any character in the team uses EX Specials, boosting Ether DMG for the equipper if the effect is activated by other party members, all the while boosting Anomaly Mastery with the 2-piece effect.
Best combined with your choice of 2-PC Set:

  • Chaos Jazz, Freedom Blues (Recommended)
  • Chaotic Metal
  • Astral Voice
  • Hormone Punk
  • Puffer Electro

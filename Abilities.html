Skills

Core skills

When Basic Attack: Featherbloom hits a target that is suffering from an Ether, Electric, Fire, Physical, or Ice Anomaly, it will trigger Abloom, dealing additional Attribute Anomaly DMG.
The additional DMG is 3.07% / 1.6% / 4% / 0.37% / 0.54% per 10 points of Anomaly Proficiency of the original Anomaly DMG.
When Basic Attack: Fluttering Frock - Suspension or Basic Attack: Featherbloom hits a target suffering any Attribute Anomaly, it will inflict Vivian's Prophecy, causing the target to take Ether DMG equal to 55% of Vivian's ATK every 0.55s.
This DMG can trigger additional DMG from Attribute Anomalies.
Vivian's Prophecy ends when the target is no longer suffering an Attribute Anomaly. When entering the battlefield, Vivian gains 2 Flight Feathers.

Additional Ability: Prophetic Tears

When another character in your squad is an Anomaly character or shares the same attribute:
Whenever another squad member applies Attribute Anomaly to an enemy,
Vivian will consume 1 Guard Feather to launch Basic Attack:
Featherbloom on the target. This can trigger once every 0.5s.
Corruption DMG dealt by all squad members and the Corruption status'
Disorder DMG increases by 12%.

Basic Attack:
Feathered Strike

Press Basic Attack to activate: Unleashes up to 4 attacks in front, dealing Physical DMG and Ether DMG. After triggering the fourth attack, enter the Noblewoman state. With Flight Feathers available, holding Basic Attack during the fourth attack will activate the Fluttering Frock state.

Basic Attack:
Noblweoman - Waltz

Press Basic Attack while in the Noblewoman state to activate: Dash forward with an attack that deals Ether DMG and restores 1 Flight Feather upon hitting an enemy. Character is invulnerable while using this skill. If a Perfect Dodge is triggered during the dash, gain 1 additional Flight Feather. If Flight Feathers are available after the skill, enter the Fluttering Frock state, which can be canceled by holding Dodge when the skill is used.

Basic Attack:
Fluttering Frock - Suspension

Press Basic Attack while in the Fluttering Frock state to activate: Attack an area ahead, dealing Ether DMG. After activation, this skill consumes all Flight Feathers, and each one consurned grants 1 Guard Feather. Character is invulnerable while using this skill. When switching characters during the Fluttering Frock state, Vivian will automatically perform Basic Attack: Fluttering Frock - Suspension. While in the Fluttering Frock state, press Dodge to exit the state.

Dodge:
Lightstream

Recomended level 10
Press Dodge to activate.A quick dash dodge. Character is invulnerable while using this skill.

Dodge Counter:
Wingblade Reverb

Recomended level 10
Press Basic Attack during a Perfect Dodge to activate. Launches an attack on enemies ahead, dealing Physical DMG and Ether DMG.Character is invulnerable while using this skill.After activating the skill, enter the Noblewoman state. When there are Flight Feathers available, hold Basic Attack during skill activation to enter the Fluttering Frock state.

Quick Assist:
Frostwing Guard

Recomended level 10
When the active character is launched, press Assist to activate. Launches an attack on enenies ahead, dealing Physical DMG and Ether DMG.
Character is invulnerable while using this skill. After the skill activates, enter the Noblewoman state. With Flight Feathers available, hold Basic Attack during skill activation to enter the Fluttering Frock state.

Defensive Assist:
Silver Umbrella Formation

Recomended level 10
When the character on field is about to be attacked, press Assist to activate. Parries the enemy's attack, dealing massive Daze. Character is invulnerable while using this skill.

Special Attack:
Song of Silver Wings

Press Special Attack to activate. Quickly attack forward, dealing Physical DMG and Ether DMG.
Anti-Interrupt level is increased while using this skill.

EX Special Attack:
Violet Requiem

Recomended level 10
With enough Energy, press Special Attack to activate. Unleash a powerful forward attack that deals massive Ether DMG and grants 3 Flight Feathers.
 Character is invulnerable while using this skill. Enter the Fluttering Frock state after using the skill. Hold Dodge during the skill to enter the Noblewoman state.

Chain Attack:
Chorus of Celestial Wings

When a Chain Attack is triggered, select the character to activate: Launch a powerful attack on a large area of enemies ahead,
dealing massive Ether DMG and gaining 2 Flight Feathers. Character is invulnerable while using this skill.
After the skill activates, enter the Fluttering Frock state, which can be canceled by holding Dodge during the skill.

Ultimate (3000 Decibel cost):
Soarding Bird's Song

Recomended level 10
When Decibel Rating is at Maximum, press Ultimate to activate. Launch a powerful attack on a large area of enemies ahead,
dealing massive Ether DMG and gaining 5 Flight Feathers.
Character is invulnerable while using this skill. After the skill is activated, enter the Fluttering Frock state,
which can be canceled by holding Dodge during the skill.

Mindscapes

Walking Into the Orchard in Spring (Mindscape 1)
When a total of 4 Guard Feathers are consumed, gain 1 Flight Feather. All Attribute Anomaly DMG and Disorder DMG received by targets under Vivian's Prophecy increases by 16%.

Stormy Night, Stormy Night (Mindscape 2)
Vivian's Ether Anomaly Buildup Rate increases by 25%. The benefits of Abloom from Anomaly Proficiency are raised to 130% of the original value, and 15% of the target's All-Attribute RES is ignored.

Another and the Same (Mindscape 3)
Basic Attack, Assist, Dodge, Special Attack, and Chain Attack Lv. +2

The Wind Between the Reeds (Mindscape 4)
Basic Attack: Fluttering Frock - Suspension and Basic Attack: Featherbloom will always CRIT on hit, and they will also increase Vivian's ATK by 12% for 12s. Repeated triggers reset the duration. When entering the battlefield, Vivian immediately gains 5 Guard Feathers.

Sonnet (Mindscape 5)
Basic Attack, Assist, Dodge, Special Attack, and Chain Attack Lv. +2

Vivian (Mindscape 6)
Vivian's Ether DMG increases by 40%. When triggering a Perfect Dodge during Basic Attack: Noblewoman - Waltz's dash, or using EX Special Attack: Violet Requiem, gain 1 additional Flight Feather. Launching Basic Attack: Fluttering Frock - Suspension consumes all Guard Feathers, the downward attack's area of effect increases, and upon hit, triggers a special instance of Abloom. Each additional Guard Feather consumed will increase the proportion of additional Anomaly DMG dealt by that instance of Abloom, consuming a maximum of 5 Guard Feathers to increase the DMG up to a maximum of 5 times the original.
